What is offline AI? A playable answer

Last updated: 28 July 2026 (v1.4) · By M-Corp Ethical AI
Offline AI (also called on-device AI or local AI) is artificial intelligence that runs entirely on the user's own device — with no cloud servers, no internet connection, and no data transmission. Because every computation stays local, offline AI protects privacy by design and keeps working in disconnected or low-resource environments.

Most pages can only describe offline AI. This page lets you run one. The game above, Wraptris, contains a complete offline AI: press 🤖 AI Hint and it evaluates every possible move directly in your browser, ranks its top-3 candidates with visible scores, and explains its reasoning — while your device stays fully disconnected from any server.

Why offline AI matters

Cloud AI has two structural risks: your data leaves your device, and the AI stops working the moment connectivity does. Offline AI removes both.

  • Privacy by design — nothing is transmitted, so there is nothing to leak, sell, or surveil.
  • Works anywhere — during disasters, in refugee camps, in rural clinics, or wherever the internet is unavailable or unsafe.
  • No legal exposure — serverless design means no data controller obligations, which is why NGOs and humanitarian organizations can adopt it.
  • Inspectable — when the AI is local and open, anyone can audit exactly what it does.

How this game proves it

Wraptris is built from a single block of vanilla JavaScript with zero external libraries. Its AI is a transparent linear heuristic — the exact formula is printed on screen: score = −0.51·height + 0.76·lines − 0.36·holes − 0.18·bumpiness. Load the page once, then disconnect from the internet entirely: the game and its AI keep working. That is offline AI, demonstrated rather than claimed.

Offline AI — frequently asked questions

What is offline AI in simple terms? AI that lives on your device instead of in the cloud. It answers, decides, or assists without sending anything over the internet.

Is offline AI less capable than cloud AI? It is differently scoped. For focused tasks — evaluation, classification, decision support — a small local model can be fast, fully private, and completely auditable, which cloud AI cannot guarantee.

Is this game really an offline AI example? Yes. After the page loads, every AI computation happens in your browser. There are no API calls, no telemetry, and no external libraries.

Is the AI a black box? No. Its weights are published on screen, its top-3 candidate moves are shown with scores, and it reports how many placements it evaluated for every decision.

Does anything leave my device? No. Your best score is stored only in your own browser. There is no tracking of any kind.
